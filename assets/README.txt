Place your logo here as logo.png (recommended: 240×60 px, transparent background).

A placeholder SVG (logo.svg) is included for reference.

For LaTeX/Beamer/PPTX output, PNG is most reliable.
To convert the SVG to PNG if you have ImageMagick:

    convert -background none logo.svg logo.png

Or use Inkscape:

    inkscape --export-filename=logo.png --export-width=240 logo.svg
